# Stories

<!--
A bank of short work stories, tagged so the drafter can pick the right one
for a posting and so you have them ready for interviews.

Rules:
- Each story lists the fact IDs it relies on. Any number, date, title or
  result in a story must come from a [confirmed] line in facts.md. A story
  that leans on an unconfirmed fact is not used in drafts.
- Tags are plain words. Reuse the same tags across stories so matching works
  (for example: inventory, process-improvement, training, conflict,
  data-analysis, vendor).
- Keep each part to one to three sentences. Detail can come out in an
  interview.
- Cover letters are built from one or two stories, so include stories that
  show how you work with people (coaching, getting a team unstuck, taking
  a call under pressure), not only results with numbers. Say what you did
  and why, in the first person.
-->

## S-01: Short title

- Tags: tag, tag
- Facts used: F-000, F-000
- Situation: What was going on and why it mattered.
- Action: What you did, specifically.
- Result: What changed. Use only confirmed numbers.
- Good for: kinds of questions or postings this answers
