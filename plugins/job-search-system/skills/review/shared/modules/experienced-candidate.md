# Experienced-candidate module

Turns on from the situation fields (long experience, a move to a lower
level, or a gap), never from age (decision 015). It changes how a draft
is shaped, not what it may claim. Every rule in
`shared/writing-rules.md` still applies.

## Resume

- **Trim by relevance and recency.** Give the most space to roughly the
  last 10 to 15 years and to anything that matches the posting's top
  requirements. Earlier roles can collapse into one line each, or into a
  short "Earlier experience" list with titles and employers.
- **Earlier dates are optional.** If the user's fact lines for early roles
  have dates, follow `voice.md`; if `voice.md` says nothing, keep dates on
  every role shown in full and leave them off the collapsed earlier roles.
  Never add a date the facts do not give.
- **Show current skills.** Put tools and methods with recent use first.
  Skills the facts mark as old ("used in 2014") go last or come out, unless
  the posting asks for them.
- **Match the level.** For a role below the user's recent level, lead with
  hands-on work and results, not size of team or budget. Do not hide past
  titles; describe the work in the posting's terms.
- **Keep it to the length in `voice.md`.** A long career is not a reason to
  go past two pages.

## Cover letter

- **Lower level.** If the target is below `recent_level`, say why in one
  plain sentence, from `answers.md` ("Why this kind of role?") when there
  is an answer. Do not apologize and do not oversell.
- **Gap.** Only if `situation.gap.explanation` has text, and in the user's
  words.
- **Answer the unasked question.** A reviewer may wonder whether the user
  will stay, take direction, or want the manager's job. If `answers.md`
  speaks to it, one sentence can address it. If not, leave it alone; do not
  invent a reason.

## What the reviewer should watch for

- Experience described so it sounds bigger than the role needs, which can
  read as overqualified.
- Old tools listed as current.
- Any phrase that points at age ("decades of", "seasoned veteran",
  "digital native"). These are blockers under `never_in_draft`.
